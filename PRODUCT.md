# Product

## Register

product

## Users

NEET UG students in India who plan and study primarily on Android phones, often on mid-range devices and around school, college, coaching, meals, sleep, and other fixed commitments. They need to understand what is realistically possible, decide which NCERT chapter to study next, and stay oriented without learning a novel interface.

## Product Purpose

NEET Planner turns the rationalised Class 11 and 12 NCERT syllabus, a target date, available study hours, and fixed commitments into an actionable daily study plan. Success means the student can choose the chapters they intend to cover, see the gap between the work and the time available, complete focused sessions, adjust later, and understand progress without manual scheduling overhead.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a precise native study tool: supportive without cheerleading, structured without becoming bureaucratic, and confident without looking promotional.

## Anti-references

- No generic dashboard made from repetitive cards.
- No neon, game-like, or reward-heavy study aesthetic.
- No marketing-page typography or decorative motion inside task flows.
- No custom controls that behave differently from familiar Material 3 controls.
- No visual drift between the Angular reference and native Android implementation.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next useful decision obvious on every screen.
2. Explain planning arithmetic in plain language and keep it traceable.
3. Use one consistent Material 3 vocabulary across onboarding and the main app.
4. Reveal detail progressively while keeping primary actions reachable on a phone.
5. Preserve the student's work locally and make destructive actions explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use at least 48 dp interactive targets, strong text and control contrast, visible selected and disabled states, meaningful accessibility labels, scalable text, non-colour state cues, and motion that respects reduced-motion preferences. All user-facing copy must be resource-backed so future translations can be added through Android string resources.
