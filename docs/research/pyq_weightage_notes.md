## Counting conventions

- 2019, 2020: 180 questions attempted (45/45/90); sources state 45 for physics/chemistry. Biology tables total 90 (NEETprep: Zoology 40 + Botany 50).
- 2021-2024 papers had 50 questions per subject (Section A 35 + Section B 15, 10 to attempt). PW 2024 tables are 'Grand Total 50' per subject (all 50 counted). Vedantu 2021/Byjus 2021 biology tables sum to 100 (all 50+50 counted). 2022/2023 counts come from multi-year grids that do not state totals (row sums are in `tables[].rowsSum`, e.g. Careers360 physics 50/50/50/50 then 45 for 2025).
- 2025: no optional section; 45 per subject attempted. PW and Vedantu physics/chemistry tables total 45. PW biology page: botany rows 49, zoology rows 41 (sum 90; the main PW 2025 page has one extra 'Biotechnology and its Applications: 4' row making 94). Vedantu: botany 50, zoology 40. Careers360 biology totals 90.
- 2026 re-exam (21 June): PW physics table sums 44 (one chapter row shows '-'), chemistry and biology tables partial. 2026 cancelled 3 May: PW tables partial (physics 39, chemistry 24).
- Where a source splits one of our chapters across rows (Rotational Motion + Center of Mass; Animal Kingdom-1 + -2; Biomolecule I + II; Ionic + Chemical equilibrium; Circular Motion -> Motion in a Plane), entries carry `componentOfMerged: true` and must be summed per (source, year, paper).
- PW places some chapters differently from our ids (Biomolecules and Biotechnology rows appear in its Zoology tables; Microbes in Human Welfare appears in its Botany table). Entries are keyed by our chapter id; the source's own placement is in `subjectSection`.

## Limitations

- All figures are from coaching-institute web pages, not NTA. NTA publishes no chapter-wise breakdown. Different sources tag the same question to different chapters; treat any single count as +/-1-2.
- 2022 and 2023 have no standalone per-paper analysis that was retrievable. PW's 2022 URL actually carries a NEET 2019 physics table (headed 'NEET 2019' in the page); recorded as 2019 with a caveat. PW 2023 uses legacy unit labels (physics rows sum to 54, not 50) and its chemistry tables are identical to an Allen average table, so chemistry was excluded.
- 2019 and 2020: only unit-level counts for most subjects (Mechanics, Optics, Human Physiology ...) are in `unmapped` with reason 'unit-level'. Chapter-level 2019 physics rests on one PW table; 2019/2020 chemistry on one Collegedunia grid (identical to PW's older grid).
- Several sources copy each other (Vedantu/Byjus/Unacademy 2021 are identical; Testbook physics 2023-2025 is identical to the PW weightage table; Collegedunia's 2019-2023 chemistry grid equals PW's older grid). Counting each page as a data point overstates independent evidence; JSON keeps them all as instructed (1885 mapped entries + 179 unmapped).
- Removed-syllabus and p-block/s-block chapters (p-block, s-block, hydrogen, metallurgy, solid state, surface chemistry, polymers, environmental chemistry, Transport in Plants, Mineral Nutrition, Digestion and Absorption, Strategies for Enhancement in Food Production, Environmental Issues) are not in our chapter list and sit under `unmapped`. 2021-2023 counts for those chapters are therefore excluded from any weighting of our chapters.
- Mapping judgements: 'Circular Motion' and 'Projectile Motion' -> Motion in a Plane; 'Centre of Mass'/'Rotational Motion' -> System of Particles and Rotational Motion; 'Surface Tension' and 'Fluid Mechanics' -> Mechanical Properties of Fluids; 'Purification and characterisation of organic compounds', 'GOC', 'IUPAC & Isomerism' -> Organic Chemistry: Some Basic Principles and Techniques; 'Organic Compounds Containing Nitrogen' -> Amines; 'Mole concept' -> Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry; 'Ionic equilibrium' -> Equilibrium; 'Biomolecule I/II' -> botany Biomolecules; 'Origin and Evolution' -> Evolution. Rows spanning two of our chapters (Optics, Electrostatics, Kinematics, Atoms & Nuclei, Magnetic Effects of Current & Magnetism, ...) are unmapped.
- Careers360's 2026 column is not labelled with a paper; kept as '2026-unspecified'. Careers360 numbers for 2025 differ a lot from PW/Vedantu (it uses its own chapter grouping).
- Partial 2026 tables: PW re-exam chemistry lists 14 chapters (sum 40/45); PW re-exam biology lists 16 chapters (sum 48) with no zoology chapters. Do not treat missing chapters in those tables as zero.
- Data was parsed from page text with a script and mapped by name aliases; spot-check before shipping. Status stays draft-unverified.
