NEET Planner ("the app") is a study planner for NEET students. This policy says
exactly what the app stores, what it sends, and how to turn sending off.

## The short version

- Your plan, syllabus progress, study sessions, revision schedule, mocks and
  mistake tags **stay on your phone**. We never receive them.
- If "Share anonymous usage data" is on (the default), the app sends **usage
  events and crash reports** to Google Firebase so we can fix bugs and improve
  the app. You can turn this off at any time in **Settings → Privacy**.
- There are **no ads**, no account and no sign-in. We do not collect the
  Advertising ID.
- Payments for Pro are handled entirely by **Google Play**.

## 1. Data stored only on your device

Everything you enter is kept in the app's private storage on your phone:

- exam, exam date, finish-by date, plan mode and starting point;
- daily study hours, fixed commitments, wake and sleep times, study place;
- syllabus choices, chapter stages, chapter renames, coaching lecture and DPP counts;
- the daily plan, Focus and manually logged study sessions, revision schedule;
- mock and test scores and mistake tags;
- app settings (theme, Focus length, reminder time, the usage-data choice);
- whether you own Pro (a cached copy of what Google Play reports).

This data is **not sent to us or to anyone else**. It is excluded from Android
cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its
data deletes it permanently.

**Export and import.** Settings → Your data → Export backup creates a JSON file at a
location *you* choose. The app does not upload it anywhere. Import only reads a
file you pick. What you do with an exported file is up to you.

**Reminders.** The optional daily reminder and the Focus timer notifications are
scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

## 2. Data sent to Google Firebase (only while sharing is on)

When "Share anonymous usage data" is on, the app uses two Google services:

**Firebase Analytics** receives:

- app interaction events, such as "onboarding completed", "block started",
  "block completed", "chapter learned", "mock logged", "paywall viewed",
  "purchase completed", "widget added" and "export done". Events carry only
  categories and counts (for example the plan mode, the activity type, the
  number of missed blocks). They never contain your name, chapter titles,
  scores, notes or any text you typed;
- events Firebase collects automatically, such as first open, sessions,
  screen views, app updates and in-app purchase events;
- an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase for this installation, and
  device information such as phone model, Android version, language and an
  approximate country/region derived by Google from the network IP address.

**Firebase Crashlytics** receives, when the app crashes or hits a fatal error:
the stack trace, the app's state at the time, device details (model, Android
version, free memory and storage, orientation) and a Crashlytics installation
identifier.

We have turned off, in the app itself:

- collection of the Android Advertising ID (and the app does not request the
  `AD_ID` permission);
- collection of the Android SSAID;
- ad personalisation signals.

Firebase sends this data over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Google processes
it on our behalf as a service provider under the
[Firebase data processing terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms).
See also [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

**Turning it off.** Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous usage data". When off,
the app stops Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collection on this device.
The choice is remembered across restarts.

## 3. Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play. Google Play handles the
payment and your payment details; the app never sees them. The app asks Google
Play whether this Google account owns Pro (on launch and when you tap "Restore
purchase") and keeps that yes/no answer on the phone so Pro works offline. We do
not run a server and do not receive your purchase or payment details. Google's
handling of payments is covered by the
[Google Play terms](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not sell data.
- We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers. There are no ads.
- We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise
  location.
- We do not use the data sent to Firebase to identify you.

## 5. Retention

On-device data stays until you delete it: clear the app's storage in Android
settings or uninstall the app ("Clear plan and restart" in Settings erases your
plan, syllabus ticks and sessions). Firebase Analytics event data is kept for the retention period
set in our Firebase project (Google Analytics default: 2 months for user-level
data). Crashlytics crash reports are kept for 90 days.

## 6. Children

The app is meant for students preparing for NEET, typically 16 and older. It is
not directed at children under 13. It shows no ads and collects no Advertising
ID.

## 7. Your choices

- Turn off usage data and crash reports: Settings → Privacy.
- Delete everything on the phone: clear the app's storage in Android settings, or
  uninstall. Settings → "Clear plan and restart" erases your plan, syllabus ticks
  and sessions.
- Ask about data sent to Firebase for your installation: write to us at the
  address below. Because we do not collect your name or email, we may need the
  details of your request to find anything.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a
new "Last updated" date, and the in-app Privacy page will be updated in the
same release.
